MSE Inspection Autofill

Privacy Policy

Summary

MSE Inspection Autofill runs entirely in your browser. It reads the Excel file you choose and fills the audit report page you have open. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. There are no analytics, tracking, or ads.

What the extension does

Auditors use the extension to fill the Internal Audit Report chapter pages on the MSE inspection portal (inspection-os.msei.in) from the Annexure II Excel template. You choose the Excel file in the extension popup, open a chapter page, and click "Fill this chapter". Nothing is filled until you click. You review the page and submit it to the portal yourself. The extension never submits forms for you.

Information the extension handles

InformationHow it is usedWhere it stays
Contents of the Excel file you choose (checkpoint wording, compliance status, sample sizes, instance counts, percentages, amounts, remarks)To fill the matching fields on the chapter pageYour browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer
Your extension settings (overwrite, default remarks)To remember your choicesLocal extension storage on your computer
The open chapter page on the MSE portal (chapter number, checkpoint wording, form fields)Read in the page so each Excel row lands in the right field, and the fields are filled thereNever stored or copied anywhere by the extension

The extension does not collect your name, email, login details, passwords, browsing history, location, or any other personal information. It does not read pages on any other website.

Sharing and transfer

  • No data is sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party.
  • No data is sold or used for advertising, credit decisions, or any purpose unrelated to filling the audit report.
  • The extension contains no remote code, analytics, or tracking scripts. All code ships inside the extension package.

When you click Save on the portal, the portal receives the filled values just as if you had typed them. That submission happens between your browser and the portal under the portal's own terms, not the extension's.

Keeping and deleting your data

The Excel data stays in local storage until you upload a different file or remove the extension. Uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions deletes all of it.

Security

Your data never leaves your computer through the extension, so its security depends on your device and browser profile. Use a device and Chrome profile that only authorised people can access.

Children

The extension is a professional tool for auditors and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the way the extension handles data changes, this page will be updated before the new version is published, and the date below will change.
